Voting

Community vote

Fixed fairness rules
  • Votes can be changed until voting closes.
  • Live totals are visible to staff, not regular players.
  • Judges cannot public-vote.
  • Owners/main members cannot vote for their entry; helpers may.
  • Guild members cannot vote for their own guild entry.
  • Default eligibility is 120 minutes of active PlayTimePlugin time.

Judging

Judge scoring

Assigned judges cannot enter or vote. They may be listed only as unrewarded helpers/contributors.

Scoring criteria

Every criterion uses a 0–10 score. Weight controls its influence within the judge component.

Moderation

Review & content safety

Staff approval Always required before an entry becomes public.
OpenAI Moderation API Always screens submitted text and images. API failure fails closed.
Minecraft privacy review Manual staff check for coordinates, waypoints, minimaps, private chat, and relevance.
Entry upload pages will clearly warn players that screenshots become public and should not expose coordinates, waypoints, minimaps, private chat, or other sensitive base information.
