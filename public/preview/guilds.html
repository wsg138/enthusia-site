HOW GUILDS WORK

More than a tag

1

Levels

Guilds earn XP together and level up from 0 to 200, unlocking rewards along the way.

2

Prestige and Seasonal ELO

New in Chapter 2. Prestige marks long-term progress, and Seasonal ELO ranks guilds against each other each season.

3

War and diplomacy

Set your guild to Peaceful or Hostile, name allies and enemies, and declare war.

4

Shared property

A guild vault, guild homes, ally homes and a guild stall in the Market.

PAST GUILDS

Gone, not forgotten

Guilds that shaped the server and are no longer active. Their stories stay on the timeline.

ScarEye

Final Open Beta

Led by RadiOwO_. Raised its banner near spawn and fought the ScarEye War against Enthusiast and Blood Guard. It did not return for full release.

OverHeaven

Final Open Beta

Formed for the End of the World on 11 April 2026 and reached the final fight against Blood Guard.

Kingsmould

First Open Beta

Called the first truce on the server, with Everlights, on 31 October 2025.

The Imperials

Chapter 1

Led by XXJAKAXX. Raided again and again through mid-August 2026, and disbanded in early September.
