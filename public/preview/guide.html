PLAYER GUIDE

Your first hour, how the server works, and every command worth knowing.

YOUR FIRST HOUR

Seven things to do first

  1. 1. Read the rulesRaiding, griefing and open PvP are allowed in the wilderness. Cheating is not. Rules
  2. 2. Open your DiaryEvery player is given a Diary on first join. Write your story in it as you go.
  3. 3. Get away from spawn and set a homeSpawn and the Market are the only protected areas. Find somewhere quiet, then run /sethome.
  4. 4. Bank your first Raw GoldRaw Gold is the currency. /deposit puts it in the bank, where it cannot be looted.
  5. 5. Join or start a guild/g menu lists the guilds. /g create [name] starts your own. Guilds
  6. 6. Link your DiscordRun /link in-game and follow the steps, to unlock linked features on the Discord and this site. Your profile
  7. 7. Claim your daily reward/daily once a day, and /rewards for the achievements you have earned. Achievements

HOW THE SERVER WORKS

The systems, in a sentence each

Raw Gold

The currency is an item: Raw Gold. Bank it with /deposit, take it out with /withdrawl and pay other players with /pay. Gold kept in shulker boxes counts towards your balance.

Richest players

The Market

Rent a stall at the Market and stock it with shops. One stall per player. Every stall and its stock can be searched from this site.

Open the Market

Guilds

Custom ranks, a guild vault, guild homes, levels, allies, enemies and wars.

Browse guilds

Reputation

After a trade or a fight, leave the other player a positive or negative commendation with /rep. False and mass rep are against the rules.

Reputation rules

Combat and the Warzone

PvP is on everywhere except Spawn and the Market. The Warzone rotates through combat metas with their own item restrictions. Check the homepage for the current meta and its next rotation.

Current Warzone meta and duel wins

Bounties

Put Raw Gold on any head with /bounty. Whoever makes the kill collects the gold, and the head.

Bounty board

Mail

EnthusiaExpress delivers letters and packages to players who are offline, paid for in Raw Gold. Start with /mail.

Death Duels

Challenge a player to a duel in the spawn arena, with your own rules, a wager and a loot vault for the winner.

Events and duels

MODS AND CLIENTS

What you can run

Performance mods such as Sodium and Iris, Simple Voice Chat, Replay Mod and schematic-only Litematica are fine. World map mods, x-ray, ESP and freecam are not.
