GET ON THE WALL

From your map to the museum

  1. 1

    Build it

    Make your map artwork on the server and fill the maps.
  2. 2

    Send it

    Mail the filled maps to the Mapart Manager with EnthusiaExpress.
  3. 3

    Review

    Staff check the piece against the rules and pick a wall for it.
  4. 4

    On display

    It hangs in the museum and on this page, with your name on it.

The submission steps follow the planned Mapart Manager inbox. Confirm them before this page goes live.
