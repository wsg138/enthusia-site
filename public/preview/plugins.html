THE WORLD

One world, built over time

Enthusia's survival world is intended to remain permanent. New construction grows beside abandoned bases, hidden infrastructure, old battle sites, and the evidence left by earlier players.

No land claims

Protection comes from distance, planning, trusted allies, and how carefully you manage information.

Vanilla foundation

Normal survival remains the center of the server, with additional systems available around it.

Room to spread out

The Overworld reaches 100,000 blocks in every direction for travel, base hunting, and long-term projects.

COMBAT

Choose how you fight

Dedicated combat areas support different styles of PvP, while the wilderness keeps the full danger of normal survival combat.

Main Warzone

A controlled arena for regular weapon combat without building or breaking.

Cobweb Box

Utility-focused combat with a limited set of placeable items.

Warpit

A resettable arena where building, digging, traps, explosives, and carts are part of the fight.

Wilderness

Full survival combat, including crystals, anchors, TNT, pearls, and the risk of losing what you carry.

BASE PRIVACY

How PieCloak works

PieCloak reduces information exposed to client-side ESP, pie-chart, and block-entity base-finding tools. It hides selected entities and blocks from a player's client when terrain or walls block legitimate visibility. They still exist and continue working normally on the server.

Within 24 blocks

Managed entities and blocks are always shown. PieCloak does not conceal nearby activity.

From 24 to 48 blocks

The server checks the view through terrain. Three occluding block samples are enough to hide a managed entity or block.

Beyond 48 blocks

Managed entities and blocks remain outside the visibility radius until the player moves close enough.

What it protects

The current managed entity list includes villagers, copper golems, armadillos, wolves, cats, ocelots, allays, bees, iron golems, snow golems, item frames, glow item frames, armor stands, and paintings. Managed blocks include campfires, soul campfires, decorated pots, bells, jukeboxes, conduits, beacons, moving pistons, shulker boxes, signs, hanging signs, banners, beds, heads, and skulls.

What it does not do

It does not hide players or ordinary base blocks.

It does not stop mob AI, farms, breeding, villagers, trades, or block behavior.

It does not protect exposed builds, player trails, shared coordinates, maps, or normal exploration.

For the best protection, build underground. Avoid relying on entities that appear in the pie chart unless they are included in the managed list above.
