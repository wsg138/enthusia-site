Avid perks and commands

Signatures & trackers

Use /sign to preview and confirm a coloured signature and personal message on the item in your hand. Use /track to count player kills, mob kills, or blocks mined. The counter stays with the item when it changes hands.

Each item can carry one signature and one tracker. Both are permanent.

In game

  • Sit or lie down with /sit and /lay.
  • Show your held item in chat with [item].
  • Use the Avid prefix, chat colours, bold, italic, and underline. Check a player’s last login with /seen.

[Devotee] : hello!

Display approved map art on shields in Java Edition.

Devotee perks and commands

Custom shields

Hold a filled map and name your design with /mapshield create. After staff approval, apply it with /mapshield apply or take it off with /mapshield remove.

The shield keeps its durability, enchantments, name, and blocking ability. The artwork is visible on Java Edition with the server resource pack.

In game

  • Pose with /spin. Share your inventory or ender chest with [inv] or [ender].
  • Use the Devotee prefix, chat colours, bold, italic, and underline. Check a player’s last login with /seen.
  • Start events with /events when Events launches, and KOTH with /start koth when KOTH launches.

Discord

Join Donor Chat, read and send messages in the in-game chat channel, and use your avatar head emoji.
