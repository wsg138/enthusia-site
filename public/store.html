Signed items

New

Put your name on your gear. /sign adds a permanent creator signature to the item in your hand, with your name in colour and your own message. You see a preview and confirm it before it becomes permanent.

/track adds a counter to an item for player kills, mob kills or blocks mined. It keeps counting when the item changes hands.

One signature and one tracker per item. Neither can be removed.

[Devotee] : hello!

Mapart shields

New

Carry your mapart into battle. Hold a filled map and run /mapshield create with a name. Once staff approve the design, /mapshield apply puts it on the shield in your hand.

The shield keeps its durability, enchantments and name, and blocks like any other. /mapshield remove takes the art off again.

Needs the server resource pack. Java Edition only: the art is not shown to Bedrock players.

SMP

  • Custom prefix in chat [Devotee]
  • Ability to use /spin
  • Put your mapart on shields with /mapshield
  • Ability to use colors in chat and format [Bold, italic or underlined]

  • Ability to use /seen command [used to see when a player was last logged in]
