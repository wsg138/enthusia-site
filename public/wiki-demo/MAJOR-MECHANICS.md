# Enthusia Wiki: Major Mechanic Priority

A mechanic is major because of how much it changes normal play, not because it has many commands, menus, or code.

## Major-mechanic test

Treat a system as major when one or more of these are true:

- it materially changes how players survive, hide, travel, fight, trade, progress, organize, or protect information;
- it changes a normal Minecraft expectation in a way players should know early;
- it affects a large share of players even if it runs automatically;
- misunderstanding it can cause a meaningful loss, missed opportunity, or unfair-feeling surprise;
- it is distinctive enough that players may choose Enthusia partly because of it;
- it has enough rules, choices, or consequences to deserve a stable page/link.

Command count is not part of this test. Automatic systems can be major.

## Major current systems

These should be prominent in the Mechanics index and have dedicated pages:

- **Survival model / server rules** — permanent semi-anarchy world, no land claims, unrestricted wilderness PvP, combat-tag restrictions.
- **PieCloak** — major base-privacy mechanic because hidden-base information and base discovery are central to an unclaimed semi-anarchy world.
- **Raw Gold economy** — physical + banked currency and payments.
- **Market** — stalls, shops, auctions, rent and player trading.
- **Guilds** — player groups, ranks, shared homes, chat, bank and vault.
- **Homes & Teleportation** — TPA, personal homes, bed/spawn travel and safety rules.
- **Warzone** — rotating PvP rules and kits.
- **Death Duels** — consensual high-risk 1v1 fights, wagers and victor's spoils.
- **Playtime** — active/AFK tracking, leaderboards and numeral progression.
- **Reputation** — player reviews and reputation-driven gameplay effects.
- **Tags, Rewards & Cosmetics** — achievement/reward progression and unlocks.
- **DiaryKeeper / Diaries** — first-join personal diary, ownership/editing rules, protected drops/storage behavior and recovery.
- **Voting** — direct Raw Gold, streak mining boosts and Vote Party.
- **Chat & Messaging** — global/private/guild/ally channels and player communication controls.

## Prominent upcoming / disabled system

- **Server Events** — large enough to remain easy to find, but it must be clearly labeled in development/not enabled rather than presented as current gameplay.

## Important supporting systems

These still deserve dedicated pages or clearly linked sections, but normally sit below the major-mechanic group:

- AutoClicker
- Sleep acceleration / phantom rest
- Spawn behavior and protections
- Java & Bedrock differences
- Trivia
- Giveaways
- Leaderboards / donor presentation

## Usually not standalone player mechanics

These should be folded into the relevant player page instead of becoming their own major page:

- implementation-only safety infrastructure;
- a single anti-glitch rule when it belongs naturally on Spawn;
- backend presentation consumers when another system owns the mechanic;
- disabled generic plugin capabilities that do not exist on Enthusia;
- staff/developer/internal tooling.

## Review rule

Before finalizing navigation, ask: **If a normal player could be surprised, lose something, change how they build/play, or specifically seek this feature out, can they find it in one or two obvious clicks?**

If not, the navigation is underrating that mechanic.
