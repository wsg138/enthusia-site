Before you submit

Submission guidelines

Community members can submit Community Builds and Mapart. Every image is reviewed before it appears here.

Show your own work. Credit everyone who helped and do not submit copied builds or art.

Keep screenshots clean. Hide private chat, coordinates, waypoints, personal information, and unrelated overlays.

Keep it appropriate. No hateful, sexual, graphic, harassing, misleading, or promotional content.

Describe it honestly. Use a clear title and explain what the image shows. Mapart must have been made on Enthusia.

Use PNG or JPEG. Images may be up to 8 MB. Metadata is rejected for privacy.

Moderation is final. Staff may deny submissions that do not fit the gallery and may remove published work when necessary.
