The loop

How events work

  1. 1 · Vote

    Players pick the event

    Before each physical event, five random events go up for vote. The one with the most votes wins the slot.

  2. 2 · Join

    Queue up in game

    Once voting closes, the join phase opens. Sign up in chat to lock in your spot before the countdown starts.

  3. 3 · Countdown

    Get to the arena

    A countdown timer runs so everyone can get ready. When it hits zero, the event begins and late entries close.

  4. 4 · Event

    Play it out

    Compete in the chosen event. Last one standing, fastest finisher, or highest score takes the win.

  5. 5 · Trophy room

    Winners get their moment

    Winners are shown off in the trophy room so the whole server can see who took the crown.

  6. 6 · Payouts

    Collect your reward

    Payouts are sent to the winners after the event wraps up. Every win also counts toward your event stats.
