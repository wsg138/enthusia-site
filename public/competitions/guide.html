Competition guide

How competitions work

Everything you need to enter, follow a round, vote, and find the final results.

Before you enter

Open the competition and read its description, rules, dates, allowed entry types, and rewards. Competition-specific rules always take priority over this general guide.

You need to sign in with Discord and have a Minecraft account linked through the Enthusia account system before you can submit or vote.

Making an entry

  1. Open the competition while submissions are open.
  2. Choose the Minecraft account that owns the entry.
  3. Select an allowed entry type: solo, group, or guild.
  4. Add a clear title and description.
  5. Add contributors when the competition allows them.
  6. Upload screenshots, choose a cover image, and submit the entry for review.

You can keep editing a draft until you submit it. If staff requests changes, the entry will reopen for the allowed correction period.

Screenshots

There is no screenshot-count limit. Upload as many as the entry needs, but keep the set useful and avoid near-duplicates.

Each file must be a PNG or JPEG no larger than 8 MB. Images are checked before they are stored. Remove coordinates, waypoints, private chat, hidden base locations, or anything else you do not want published.

Staff review

Staff checks submitted entries for the competition rules, image safety, and required information. Approved entries continue to voting or judging. If changes are needed, the entry page will explain what to fix.

Community voting

If community voting is enabled, approved entries become visible when voting opens. Sign in, review the entries, and use the ballot shown on the competition page. The competition page lists how many choices you have and whether you can change them before voting closes.

You cannot vote with an unlinked account. Entry owners, main participants, judges, and guild members may also be restricted from voting for their own entry.

Judging

Some competitions use assigned judges instead of, or alongside, community voting. Judges score the published criteria. Private judge notes stay private; any feedback published with the results is shown without the judge’s identity.

Results and rewards

Results are published only after staff completes the final checks. The finished competition page keeps placements, approved entries, public feedback, and published rewards together so they remain easy to find later.

Competition stages

Getting ready
The rules and schedule are available before entries open.
Submissions
Players create, edit, and submit entries.
Entry review
Staff checks entries and asks for any required changes.
Community voting
Eligible players vote for approved entries when enabled.
Judging
Assigned judges score approved entries when enabled.
Final checks
Staff confirms the standings and prepares the results.
Results published
Placements are final and remain available in the archive.
